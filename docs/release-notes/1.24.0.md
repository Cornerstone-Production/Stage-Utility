Baptisms are rebuilt. The Baptisms tab reads like History: a session chart
drawn from every press, the people, past sessions and trends, with counts of
people actually baptized. The baptism phase arms and waits for the first person
in, so the band's intro is not counted. A service's History page gains a
Baptisms card with each person's times, Rebuild from raw restores a service's
sessions from the raw log, and layouts, automation and Companion can drive the
timer.

Back-to-back services on one plan split the moment the next service's first
item goes live. Automation rules and action buttons are checked as they are
saved: a rule with a field that needs attention is saved turned off and says
which. Captions show as soon as ProdCom sends them, and every channel is listed
for colors. A page whose data could not load now says so instead of looking
empty.
