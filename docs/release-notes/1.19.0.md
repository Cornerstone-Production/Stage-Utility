A console runs on a Ross Ultritouch panel. The layout editor has a canvas preset
for each panel at its exact pixels with Letterbox fit locked, a strip starter of
cue buttons and a countdown, and an Ultritouch page under Integrations covering
the DashBoard side.

The cue button is a new layout object. It binds to any cue, including pairs
hidden from Home Assistant, and shows what the device is doing: on, off,
settling after a press, stale when Companion has lost it, dimmed when the cue
refuses. A tap presses the opposite half or fires a lone cue; a refusal shows on
the button. On a wall display it draws and does nothing.

Companion import reads Open and Closed as a pair. Rule adds, edits and deletes
are logged. ProdCom backs off like every other integration, Shure chargers stop
reprinting healthy state, and the editor's widget palette floats from Add object.
