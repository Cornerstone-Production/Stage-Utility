Video feeds arrive. A Video feeds page brings camera and encoder streams into
the app through a built-in relay, so a display or a custom layout can show a
live feed. Each feed reports its health, cameras are checked while the page is
open, and feeds export and import on their own.

ScriptView is now ServiceCue, and it reads better from across the room: notes
in full white, item notes at the size of the department notes, the key and BPM
line in a lighter Branding accent, and the song's time signature beside the
BPM. The ServiceCue page can step to another plan without moving the screens,
and its text size goes from 50% to 300%, with `?text=` setting it on a
display.

The app has a new icon, and the browser tab shows the logo uploaded under
Branding. Automation rules gain a Run button, SPL meters mark their peaks, and
History, Screens, Home and Advanced open faster on a slow connection.

## Changed

- Display clocks on the Dashboard, Stage display, SPL rundown and ServiceCue header follow the 12h/24h setting, which starts at 24h.
- Running an automation rule by hand over the API is `POST /api/automation/rules/:id/run`, from the operator console or with an API token. `/test` is gone.
- Planning Center ids in an API request are numbers; anything else is refused with a 400.

## Improved

- On the ServiceCue page, A+ and A− update `?text=` in the address, so a refresh keeps the size.
- After an update, the video relay's previous version and its download are removed.
