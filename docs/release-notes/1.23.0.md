Transcription captions come back on their own. A ProdCom connection that opens,
answers every heartbeat and then delivers nothing used to leave the captions
display silently empty while the integration card read connected. The app now
notices, retries the connection a second way, and falls back to the stream that
works, saying in the log which one is carrying.

Every clock reads the server's. Displays were behind by however long an update
took to arrive, and the wall clock, stream elapsed and the projected service end
were not corrected at all. A panel whose own clock is hours out now reads right.

The History tab compares a Sunday honestly while it is still running: a
part-finished day against the same services on earlier days, a finished day
against whole days, and the day in progress builds as the room fills. The range
control also picks how far back the comparison reaches.
