Stage messages arrive. A producer sends a short message from a console to groups
of screens (Green room, Stage, Booth, or everyone) and it shows on every screen in
those groups. An alert takes those screens over for 30 seconds, and a console in
the group answers with one tap. Groups, quick messages and quick replies live
under Settings → Messages; automation rules, console Action buttons and Companion
can send messages too.

Video feeds over HLS catch back up to live after a stall, and a WebRTC screen now
says when it is holding delay in its own buffer, so a lagging feed can be traced
to the screen or to what feeds it. Rule fields that take several choices, such as
Day of week, are a real multi-select.

## Improved

- The Message composer and Messages widgets arrive at a size that fits them when added from the palette.
- Companion's stage messages actions, feedback, variables and presets come with the Companion module update that ships alongside this release.
