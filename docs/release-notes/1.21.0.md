History is rebuilt around one chart. Attendance and sound share a module that
plots the recording with the plan's items on the time axis, a stat strip in
place of the tooltip and a Customize menu in place of the chip rows, and it
grows smoothly while a service is live. The service page has a sticky header
with six figures, per-item time corrections kept as an overlay over the raw
recording, and Rebuild from raw. All services leads with Trends per service
type, with operator milestones, above a calendar shaded by how many services a
day recorded.

A cue button drives OBS, REAPER and ProVideoPlayer recording and streaming
without an automation rule, red while on and green in standby. Connect YouTube
is a button and a code typed on a phone. A panel that buffers the event stream
can poll instead, and the ProdCom client re-tries its WebSocket on a timer.
