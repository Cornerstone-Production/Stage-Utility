The history chart can carry the room's sound level. Right-click the attendance
trend, on History or on Home's Recent services card, to add a second line: each
service's level on its own dB scale, from the Smaart metric you choose. A service
with no recording breaks the line rather than reading as silence. The History
overview reads the line the same way it reads attendance, with an average level
and the change against the prior weekends.

Home's cards take their choices from the right-click menu. Recording picks its
recorder and Streaming picks its platform, so one card each stands in for the six
there were, and the Sound level card can be pinned to a single meter. Around that,
a round of fixes to the layout editor, scores, ScriptView and the status tiles.

## Changed

- Home's picker offers one Recording card and one Streaming card. Right-click to choose OBS, REAPER or every recorder; Resi, YouTube or any platform. Cards already placed keep drawing exactly what they did.
- Recording gains an Elapsed time sub-line, matching the clock on Streaming.
- Home's picker no longer lists the wall widgets whose Home card it already has, and a wall no longer lists Home's cards.
- The Sound level card on Home reads the loudest meter unless pinned. A pinned meter that stops reporting says so rather than showing another meter's level.

## Improved

- Layout editor: dragging one edge of a widget leaves the opposite edge where it was. The Align pad shows the alignment the object actually renders with. The top Save button also saves an edited mic-slots layout, and the unsaved pill knows about it.
- A status tile holds its size when it goes live, so LIVE stays the same height as RECORDING beside it.
- Right-click submenus open toward the side with room, left or upward when the screen edge is near.
- Scores: a score attaches to the game that scored, and a finished game leaves the strip.
- ScriptView's rundown fills its page edge to edge.
- The bar switch hugs its buttons.
- Opening a slots view's editor from Screens shows its slots straight away.
- A stage plot added to a layout reaches the open display, and a download that fails once is retried.
